# Regional package pricing review — September 2026

This file records the commercial basis for Shiel's regional package pricing. It is not client-facing copy.

## Principles

- Prices are set deliberately in each local currency rather than converted from the Irish price list using live FX.
- Package scope follows the recurring compliance and bookkeeping workload in that jurisdiction.
- Government filing fees, audit work, regulated corporate-service work and unusually complex international work are excluded unless explicitly stated.
- Published competitor prices are used as a market sense-check, not copied.
- Review this file and `lib/regional-packages.ts` at least every six months or when a material tax/compliance change affects workload.

## United Kingdom — GBP

2026 public-market checks:
- UK contractor packages commonly sit around £60–£150/month.
- Broader limited-company packages commonly sit around £89–£289/month, with more advisory-led packages higher.

Shiel:
- Sole Trader — £65/month
- LTD Starter — £125/month
- Contractor — £109/month
- LTD Growth — £195/month

Rationale:
- Contractor and LTD entry prices sit inside current public-market bands.
- The scope explicitly includes CT600/statutory accounts and separates Companies House filing fees where relevant.
- Growth pricing allows room for payroll and quarterly management reporting.

Reference URLs:
- https://www.gov.uk/prepare-file-annual-accounts-for-limited-company
- https://www.gov.uk/guidance/filing-your-companys-confirmation-statement
- https://accounts-os.com/blog/contractor-accountant-cost-uk-2026
- https://limitedcompany.accountants/guides/how-much-does-an-accountant-cost-limited-company

## UAE — AED

2026 public-market checks:
- Published Dubai/UAE monthly accountancy offers span roughly AED 500 to AED 5,000+ depending on volume and reporting.
- A current published competitor offers an SME starter from AED 790/month and a growth package from AED 1,490/month.

Shiel:
- Freelancer — AED 590/month
- Business Starter — AED 790/month
- Business Growth — AED 1,490/month
- Business Scale — AED 2,490/month

Rationale:
- Starter and Growth are directly within current published local price bands.
- Freelancer is deliberately lean and volume-capped.
- Scale remains below premium outsourced-finance pricing while covering heavier bookkeeping and management reporting.

## Gibraltar — GIP

Public fixed-fee package pricing is sparse compared with the UK and UAE. Local firms commonly publish service scope rather than a fixed monthly rate.

Shiel:
- Self-Employed — £95 GIP/month
- Company Starter — £175 GIP/month
- Company Growth — £295 GIP/month
- Company Scale — £495 GIP/month

Rationale:
- Pricing is constructed from the expected bookkeeping, CT1, accounts, payroll and reporting workload rather than presented as a Gibraltar market average.
- The local price display uses Gibraltar pounds (GIP), not an FX conversion.
- Audit work, registry fees and regulated/licensed corporate-service work remain separately scoped.

Reference URLs:

## Spain — EUR

2026 public-market checks:
- Public guides place basic autónomo gestor/accounting support commonly around €50–€100/month, depending on service depth.
- Company work is materially more involved because bookkeeping, IVA and Impuesto sobre Sociedades obligations sit alongside other recurring filings.

Shiel:
- Autónomo Essentials — €69/month
- Autónomo Plus — €119/month
- SL Starter — €175/month
- SL Growth — €295/month

Rationale:
- Autónomo Essentials sits within the common public-market range.
- Plus adds capacity and additional recurring filing support.
- SL prices reflect the additional bookkeeping and company-tax workload rather than copying autónomo pricing.

Reference URLs:
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/libros-registro.html
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/impuestos-tasas/impuesto-sobre-sociedades.html
- https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/procedimientos/GE04.shtml
- https://getrenn.com/blog/autonomo-costs

## Review triggers

Re-review pricing immediately if any of the following happens:
- local filing frequency or digital-reporting rules materially change;
- software or filing costs become part of the package scope;
- average onboarding or monthly service time materially exceeds the assumed workload;
- the first 20 clients in a region consistently hit overage limits;
- statutory filing fees change enough to affect client expectations;
- a region begins requiring additional regulated/local representation for work currently described as included.
